Victoria (Qiyue) Zhu

I am a master's student in Software Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. I work on reliable AI systems and build software that turns research ideas into useful tools.

My research has centered on log intelligence: understanding where LLM-based parsers fail, retrieving related patterns, and consolidating fragmented templates. I also enjoy the engineering work around an idea, from data pipelines and evaluation to interfaces that people can actually use.

Research interests

01 / Focus

Reliable AI systems

Studying failure modes and improving the consistency of systems that depend on model outputs, with careful evaluation alongside implementation.

Log intelligence

Using semantic retrieval, structural matching, and feedback from parsing errors to turn noisy machine logs into useful templates.

Research into software

Building the data and application layers around an idea so its methods can be tested, understood, and used beyond a prototype.

Publications

2025

EM-Merge: Consolidating Fragmented Templates in LLM Log Parsing

Qiyue Zhu, Jinyuan Wang, Yani Yang, and Tong Li · SETTA 2025, Springer LNCS

Semantic candidate retrieval and confidence-weighted structural merging for fragmented log templates. Evaluated on 14 log datasets, with 22.1% lower processing cost reported on OpenSSH and HealthApp.

Published paper ↗Author manuscript ↗Code ↗

Also working on KnowledgeLog, a knowledge-guided approach to diagnosing and improving LLM log parsing. Manuscript in preparation.
