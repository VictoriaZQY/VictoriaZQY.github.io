EM-Merge: Consolidating Fragmented Templates in LLM Log Parsing

Qiyue Zhu, Jinyuan Wang, Yani Yang, and Tong Li

EM-Merge consolidates fragmented templates from LLM-based log parsers through semantic candidate retrieval and confidence-weighted structural merging. The work aims to improve parsing quality while reducing repeated processing.
