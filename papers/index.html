SETTA 2025 · Springer LNCS

EM-Merge: Consolidating Fragmented Templates in LLM Log Parsing

Qiyue Zhu, Jinyuan Wang, Yani Yang, and Tong Li

EM-Merge uses semantic candidate retrieval and confidence-weighted structural merging to consolidate fragmented templates produced by LLM log parsers. The work evaluates parsing quality and processing cost across 14 log datasets.
